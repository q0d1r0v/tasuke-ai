FULL DESCRIPTION  (App Store description: 4000 max / Play full: 4000 max)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Say it once. Tasuke AI writes it down.

"Tomorrow at 3 PM send the build to James and Friday check App Store."

One sentence. Two tasks, each with the right date, each with a reminder set. No
typing, no tapping through a date picker, no deciding where the first task ends
and the second begins.

EVERYTHING HAPPENS ON YOUR PHONE

Tasuke AI runs its speech recognition and its task extraction on your device.
Your voice is never uploaded. Your task list is never uploaded. There is no
account to create, no server to trust and no data for anyone to lose, because
none of it ever leaves your phone.

It works in airplane mode. It works on a plane, in a lift, in a basement, on a
train through a tunnel — which is exactly where you remember the thing you were
supposed to do.

WHAT IT DOES

• Speak naturally — several tasks in one breath, and it splits them correctly
• Understands real dates — "tomorrow at 3", "next Friday", "in two weeks",
  "the 14th", "Monday morning"
• Shows you everything before it saves, so nothing lands in your list unchecked
• Reminders that arrive on time, even with the app closed
• Search across everything you have ever captured
• Groups your day into Today, Tomorrow, This Week and Later
• Light and dark, and it follows your system setting

WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

• No account. No email address. No password.
• No analytics. No crash reporting. No advertising. No tracking of any kind.
• No cloud sync, because there is no cloud.
• Nothing to download after you install it. The speech model is built in, so
  voice capture, tasks and reminders work with the internet off from the very
  first launch.

FREE, AND HONESTLY FREE

One capture every day, spoken or typed, permanently. Unlimited reminders,
unlimited search, unlimited history — those are never limited.

TASUKE PRO

Unlimited captures, for people who put their whole day into their phone.

• $4.99 per month
• $39.99 per year (save 33%)

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the
end of the current period. Payment is charged to your store account at
confirmation of purchase. Manage or cancel in your store account settings at any
time.

Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are linked in the app, and bundled inside it so
they open without a connection.
